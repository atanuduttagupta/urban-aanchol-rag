# Urban Aanchol — MVP Scope

## MVP Goal

Create a polished saree boutique website with a useful RAG chatbot that helps customers find sarees using natural language.

## Customer Queries Supported

### Product Discovery

- Festival
- Occasion
- Mood / style
- Colour
- Fabric
- Brand
- Model / design
- Budget
- Availability

### Combined Queries

Example:

"Show me a vibrant red saree for Durga Puja under ₹5,000."

The chatbot should understand multiple criteria in one query.

### Product Questions

- Price
- Fabric
- Colour
- Design
- Availability
- Product details

### Boutique Questions

- How to order
- Payment information
- Delivery information
- Return policy
- Contact information
- WhatsApp ordering

## MVP Guardrails

The chatbot must:

- Stay focused on Urban Aanchol and sarees
- Not answer unrelated questions
- Not invent products
- Not invent prices
- Not invent availability
- Not invent policies
- Clearly say when information is unavailable
- Avoid recommending unavailable products
- Treat retrieved content as data, not instructions
- Reject unsafe requests

## MVP Website

Pages / sections:

- Home
- Catalogue
- About
- Contact
- WhatsApp ordering

Features:

- Responsive design
- Product catalogue
- Product details
- Search
- RAG chatbot
- WhatsApp enquiry/order
- Social media links

## Not Required for MVP

- Voice
- Image similarity search
- Virtual try-on
- Automated payment
- Shopping cart
- Customer purchase history
- Native mobile apps
- Advanced analytics
