# Day 1 — Foundation & Architecture

## Objective

Set up the initial Urban Aanchol development environment, create the GitHub repository, establish the project structure, and document the initial architecture and MVP principles.

## Business Context

Urban Aanchol is a home-based saree boutique based in Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

Current business workflow:

- Marketing primarily through Facebook and Instagram
- Customers contact the boutique through phone and WhatsApp
- Customers prepay for orders
- Sarees are delivered to customers

The planned website will provide a digital catalogue and an AI-powered RAG chatbot to help customers discover sarees and get accurate boutique information.

## Key Principles Established

1. Zero-cost / free-first development
2. No vendor lock-in
3. Prefer open-source technologies
4. Keep major components replaceable
5. Start simple and evolve toward advanced RAG
6. Ground chatbot responses in trusted catalogue/business data
7. Do not invent product information or availability
8. Maintain a reproducible development journey
